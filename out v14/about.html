Saheli Fashion | Ahmedabad Studio

We are Heena Ghelani and Nisha Devani—two creative visionaries bringing together handcrafted Indian heritage, luxury sarees, designer lehengas, and bespoke ethnic couture for women across Ahmedabad.

Our studio is rooted in story, craftsmanship, and confidence. Each collection reflects a celebration of Gujarati tradition, royal elegance, and the personal styling experience women deserve.

From festive wardrobes to bridal selections and tailored couture, we curate attires that honor heritage while empowering every woman to feel graceful, radiant, and beautifully herself.

Heritage Craftsmanship

Kutchi & Bandhani embroideries, rich royal fabrics, and intricate handwork that celebrate Indian tradition with elevated elegance.

Bespoke Perfection

Custom fitting, direct studio experience, and personal styling that ensure every silhouette feels truly yours.

Empowering Elegance

Celebrating women, confidence, and graceful self-expression through authentic ethnic luxury for every occasion.
